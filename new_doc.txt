ABOUT US
Your Manufacturing Partner from Concept to Commercial Success
Choosing a manufacturing partner is one of the most important decisions a brand makes, because your product's quality, consistency and market readiness rest in their hands. Accra Pac has carried that responsibility as a pioneer of aerosol manufacturing in India, and is now a trusted global OEM and private label partner for aerosol, liquid and cosmetic products.
We partner with brands from the first idea to the final export shipment, helping global brands, private label companies and ambitious entrepreneurs build products ready to compete anywhere.
Our Journey
* 1979: Accra Pac is established in Vapi, Gujarat, with one of India's first state-of-the-art automatic aerosol plants.
* 1980s–1990s: Introduces ozone-friendly LPG propellant technology and expands into liquids, creams, lotions and semi-solids.
* 2005: Ships its first major export order and begins long-term relationships with international markets.
* 2010s: Adds filling lines, a dedicated roll-on line, BOV capability and in-house analytical testing.
* 2020s: Earns ISO, WHO-GMP and US FDA credentials and expands exports across the Middle East, Africa and Asia.
* Today: Supports brands from first formulation to global export.
Why Global Brands Choose Accra Pac
Proven with leading names. Brands such as Reckitt Benckiser, Henkel, Colgate, Hindustan Unilever, GSK, 3M, Godrej and Philips have tested us, trusted us and returned.
One partner, every step. Formulation, filling, packaging, regulatory documentation, testing, warehousing and export support happen under one roof, so you never manage five vendors.
Built for international standards. Our certifications and quality systems are made for regulated markets. See Quality & Compliance.
Your ideas stay yours. Strict confidentiality and secure manufacturing practices protect every formulation and project.
Why Manufacturing in India?

* Established export infrastructure and shipping networks
* English-speaking business environment and a reliable supply chain

CDMO
Contract Development and Manufacturing, Under One Roof
Some brands arrive with a finished formulation, others with an idea. As a CDMO, we take your product from concept and formulation through testing, scale-up and commercial manufacturing, so you work with one partner from first brief to final shipment.
What We Offer
* Concept & Feasibility: We turn your idea or brief into a clear development plan covering formulation approach, format, packaging and regulatory considerations.
* Custom Formulation Development: New formulations from scratch, or an adaptation of one from our ready library.
* Analytical Testing: In-house GC, HPLC and microbiology testing to validate every formulation.
* Stability Testing: Stability and shelf-life evaluation, including six flame-proof chambers for aerosol products, plus packaging compatibility testing.
* Regulatory & Documentation Support: COA, SDS and technical documentation for domestic sale and export.
* Scale-Up & Commercial Manufacturing: Validated pilot batches, then scheduled production on our qualified aerosol and liquid lines.
CDMO Workflow
1. Idea & Brief
2. Feasibility
3. Formulation Development
4. Prototype Sampling
5. Analytical & Stability Testing
6. Customer Approval
7. Scale-Up & Pilot Batch
8. Commercial Manufacturing
9. Quality Release & Dispatch
Why CDMO With Accra Pac
Development and manufacturing are usually handled by different companies, and the handoff is where delays and surprises happen. Here, the team that develops your product is the team that manufactures it, with one quality system and one point of contact from first sample to commercial batch.

Our Quality Process (graphical flow)
1. Raw Material Inspection: Every raw material and packaging component is checked before it enters production.
2. In-Process Control: Quality checks at every stage of filling and assembly.
3. Finished Product Testing: Each batch is tested before release.
4. Batch Release: Only approved batches move to dispatch.
5. Documentation: Records are completed and filed for every order.
Testing & Validation
Every formulation is validated before commercial production, using in-house GC and HPLC analysis, microbiology testing, stability and shelf-life evaluation in six flame-proof chambers, and packaging compatibility testing.
Traceability & Documentation
Every batch can be traced from raw material to production, testing, finished product and dispatch. COA, SDS and batch records are maintained for each commercial order.
