Our Journey

* 1980s–1990s — Introduces ozone-friendly LPG propellant technology and expands into liquids, creams, lotions and semi-solids, becoming one of the first Indian manufacturers to offer aerosol, liquid, cream, lotion, deo stick and sachet formats under one roof.
* 2005 — Ships its first major export order and begins a long-term export relationship with international markets.
* 2010s — Adds new filling lines, a dedicated roll-on line, and BOV (Bag-on-Valve) capability; builds out in-house GC and HPLC analytical testing.
* 2020s — Crosses 750+ products developed for 100+ customers; achieves ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 22716 and ISO 45001 certification alongside WHO-GMP and US FDA registration; expands export markets across the Middle East, Africa and Asia.
* Today — Operates 5 aerosol and 6 liquid filling lines with 120+ million units of annual capacity, supporting brands from first formulation to global export.

Most brands don't work with one manufacturer, they juggle a formulation lab, a filling contractor, a packaging vendor and a compliance consultant, and then manage the handoffs between all four. Accra Pac was built to remove that complexity. Aerosol formulation, liquid processing, filling, packaging, quality testing and export documentation all happen inside one facility in Vapi, Gujarat, so you get a single point of contact and one quality system governing your entire product, not four different ones to reconcile.


Manufacturing Technologies
* Propellant systems: Hydrocarbon, Compressed Gas, BOV (Bag-on-Valve)
* Filling & product formats: Aerosols, Liquids, Roll-Ons, Trigger Sprays, Pump Sprays, Foams, Mousses, Bottles
Aerosol Manufacturing 
Our aerosol manufacturing line covers the full process: formulation, propellant filling, can filling, valve fitting, crimping, leak testing and final quality inspection. We work with hydrocarbon and compressed-gas propellant systems, with BOV (Bag-on-Valve) technology available for formulations that need product-propellant separation. Aerosol can sizes range from 1.5 oz to 16 oz, giving brands flexibility from travel-size SKUs to full retail formats.
Liquid Manufacturing 
Our liquid manufacturing capability spans mixing, batch processing and precision filling from 2 ml up to 5 L, supporting everything from concentrated cleaning formulations to ready-to-use personal care liquids. Every batch goes through the same in-process quality checks as our aerosol lines before it moves to packaging.
Technologies & Formats
We work with LPG and hydrocarbon, DME, CO2, nitrogen, HFA-134a and HFA-152a propellants, delivered through aerosols, BOV, pump sprays, roll-ons and trigger sprays. Formulations can be water-based or alcohol-based, filled into steel or aluminium cans, plastic or glass bottles and roll-on packs. We also manufacture creams, lotions, semi-solids and sachets, and our team will help you choose the right combination for your product.
Packaging Solutions 
We handle primary packaging (cans, bottles, roll-ons, trigger and pump spray assembly), secondary packaging, labeling, artwork support and export-ready packaging, so your product leaves the plant shelf-ready and shipment-ready at the same time. Packaging development is coordinated with our R&D and quality teams from the sampling stage, so there are no compatibility surprises once a formula moves to commercial volumes.
